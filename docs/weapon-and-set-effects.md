# RU weapon and equipment effects — 2026-10-13

Sources: RU client-localized descriptions retained in catalog.json (TLSkillPcLooks_Item / TLItemSetBonusPreview). Bellandir's description was also compared against the live TL Build builder at https://tl-build.ru/builder on 2026-10-13: 20% on crossbow critical hit, 110% per second, 5 seconds, cap 3. This comparison validates the tooltip, not measured combat behavior.

Permanent set rows are applied once at their piece threshold. Missing preview rows for offhand chance, healing, debuff duration and shield block now contribute. Critical-damage set bonuses use the highest value rather than stacking. Attribute conditions are evaluated after gear, runes and mastery; max HP/MP effects are evaluated after level and attribute bonuses. Vanguard +30 affects the main crossbow only, not the offhand or wand. Mana and HP coefficients use complete 1,000-point steps and their client caps.

Supported combat effects: Bellandir poison; poisoned offhand bolts; Tevent curse; Brauka explosion; Step's Quick Fire reset; Giant's barrage multiplier; wand Circle and Beam multipliers; Quick Fire's extra projectile (one target, no ricochets); 6-second max-damage/mana window with 30-second cooldown; day/night attack speed. Conditional set damage uses the configured target distance and health. These conditions remain fixed for the simulation, not an inferred boss health curve. Elder's DoT stacks and conditional cooldown procs use expected applications.

ProcDot branches actual proc probabilities, capped stack counts, refreshed expiry and tick phase. It does not assume full stacks just because an attack can proc. Bellandir uses crossbow crit rating, including offhand critical bonuses for optional offhand attacks; wand hits and secondary damage procs do not apply it. Both autoattacks and eligible direct crossbow skills can proc it. Refresh keeps tick phase in this model; the RU client's actual refresh timer behavior still needs a combat measurement. This matters during rapid attacks and is disclosed in coverage.

The RU Curse Burst description explicitly consumes remaining burn, curse and poison damage only when the caster's curse is present. Poison alone does not enable the explosion. Remaining damage is attributed separately to Bellandir/bolt/Tevent explosions, without another crit/heavy roll or resistance multiplier. Dark Magic halves this portion and preserves DoTs. Unlucky Clock extends curses, not poison; crossbow weakening-duration extension can extend both. Fire-charge burning is now included, using the live RU builder description of Fireball: 3.4% base damage + 15 per second, 12 seconds, cap 10, 80% application chance. Curse Burst includes its residual damage.

Healing, shield lifetime, low-health weapon healing, target mana burning and defensive counterattacks now use explicit fight conditions. Party movement and reward bonuses remain character/utility effects and do not invent extra single-target damage. The incoming-damage setting is damage after mitigation, so player armor/evasion do not reduce it a second time. Automatic defensive actions assume the supplied successful-block schedule; stamina and their animation opportunity cost are not measured. The tests verify formulas and conservation of damage; they do not establish an exact RU game-client DPS match.
